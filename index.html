01 / ABOUT

Engineering knowledge.
Practical impact.

A brief overview of my academic background and professional journey.

I am an Electrical Engineering academician with interests spanning renewable energy systems, microgrid operation, power electronics and intelligent control.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore, in 2026. My doctoral work examined the performance of optimal power management and control in microgrids under uncertainties, with a focus on renewable-energy-based systems.

My professional experience includes university teaching, research activities, laboratory instruction, student project mentoring and academic development. I am particularly interested in connecting power engineering with optimization, AI/ML and real-time simulation.
